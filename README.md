# Stitchology: Your Sewing Companion

Create a mobile-first sewing companion web app named Stitchology:

Turn sewing ideas into real projects with patterns and step by step tutorials!

Learn, repair, upcycle, and create. —your simple sewing companion.

Whether you’re starting your first sewing project, repairing a favorite piece of clothing, or turning an idea into something new, it provides clear guidance every step of the way.

Explore beginner-friendly tutorials, discover creative DIY projects, generate starter sewing pattern drafts from photos, and ask the AI Sewing Coach whenever you need help.

Start your free trial and discover practical sewing guidance made for beginners, hobbyists, makers, and DIY fashion lovers.

WHAT YOU CAN DO WITH STITCHOLOGY

STEP-BY-STEP SEWING TUTORIALS

Learn sewing with clear, visual instructions designed for beginners.

Practice useful skills such as:

• Sewing straight seams
• Hemming clothes
• Adding zippers
• Making bags and accessories
• Creating simple garments
• Completing home decor projects
• Repairing and altering clothing

Each tutorial breaks the project into manageable steps, helping you understand what to do next and build confidence as you sew.

CREATE A PATTERN DRAFT FROM A PHOTO

Found a garment, bag, or design idea you love?

Take a photo and let Stitchology help you create a starter sewing pattern draft or project guide inspired by the design.

Use the generated result as a creative starting point, then adjust the measurements, shape, fabric, and construction details to suit your size and sewing experience before cutting your material.

UPCYCLING IDEAS AND DIY INSPIRATION

Give old clothes, leftover fabric, and unused materials a second life.

Discover creative ideas for repairing, redesigning, and reusing what you already have.

Try projects such as:

• Turning jeans into a skirt
• Transforming shirts into bags
• Making accessories from fabric scraps
• Restyling old clothing
• Creating simple fabric home decor
• Repairing damaged garments

ASK THE AI SEWING COACH

Not sure how to complete a sewing step? Ask the AI Sewing Coach for guidance anytime.

Get help with common sewing questions, including:

• Fixing uneven or skipped stitches
• Choosing thread, needles, or fabric
• Understanding pattern instructions
• Repairing a stuck zipper
• Adjusting a project for different materials
• Troubleshooting sewing mistakes
• Deciding what to do next
